## 🔑 GET YOUR WHATSAPP SESSION

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://teddyxmdpair-60b69f1c4cee.herokuapp.com/">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/GET-WHATSAPP_SESSION-EA580C?style=for-the-badge&logo=whatsapp&logoColor=white" alt="Get Session"/>
  </a>
</p>

1. Open the pairing website.
2. Enter your WhatsApp number.
3. Follow the pairing instructions.
4. Copy your generated session ID.
5. Add it to your hosting environment variables as `SESSION_ID`.

> ⚠️ Keep your session ID private. Never share it publicly.
